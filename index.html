Why We Fish With Purpose

Turning an Ecological Threat into Shelter Nutrition

The Wels catfish (Silurus glanis) is an invasive apex predator that devastates native river biodiversity. Under environmental regulations, captured specimens cannot be released—yet too often they are wasted. We close the loop.

Step 01

Targeted Spinning Fishing

Using active lure-fishing (spinning) techniques, Michelangelo and Lorenzo selectively target invasive Wels catfish without harming protected native fish species or using indiscriminate nets.

Step 02

Ecosystem Restoration

Removing large apex predators relieves immense predation pressure on native carp, chub, barbel, and amphibians, helping local river ecosystems regain their natural ecological balance.

Step 03

Zero-Waste Shelter Nutrition

Rather than leaving fish to rot on the banks, every catch is carefully filleted, deboned, and delivered free of charge to local dog and cat shelters (canili and gattili) as rich, lean protein.

The People Behind The Mission

Meet the Founders

Two passionate spinning anglers united by deep respect for freshwater nature and a determination to help abandoned dogs and cats.

Michelangelo Nalli — Co-Founder Lorenzo Gentili — Co-Founder

"We couldn't stand seeing river ecosystems damaged while shelters struggled to afford quality food."

As dedicated spinning fishermen, Michelangelo Nalli and Lorenzo Gentili witnessed firsthand how rapidly invasive Wels catfish were multiplying in local rivers, preying heavily on native fish populations.

When catching these powerful predators—whether compact specimens or river giants—they refused to let the catch go to waste. Recognizing that Wels catfish meat is lean, mild, and packed with essential amino acids and Omega-3s, they established River Guardians: a direct bridge between ethical river conservation and local animal shelters (canili and gattili).

Precision Spinning

Every Size Counts for Conservation

From agile juvenile catfish to heavy river adults, active night spinning allows selective capture across all size classes. Every specimen is handled hygienically, chilled immediately, and prepared safely for shelter kitchens.

Why Catfish Meat is Ideal for Rescue Dogs & Cats

Animal shelters face constant budget strain for high-quality wet food and fresh protein. Wels catfish provides a clean, easily digestible nutritional boost for shelter residents—at zero cost to the facility.

Rich in Omega-3 & Protein

Supports coat health, joint recovery, and muscle mass in undernourished rescues.

Safe & Boneless Prep

Carefully filleted without small pin bones, ready for shelter staff to boil or steam.

Ethical & Pet Safety Protocol

All Wels catfish catches strictly comply with local freshwater biodiversity regulations. Fish meat donated to canili and gattili is cleaned, filleted boneless, kept refrigerated, and intended to be thoroughly cooked by shelter staff prior to serving to dogs and cats.
